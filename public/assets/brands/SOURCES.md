# Brand logo sources

The brand-color marks are used only to identify supported vehicle makes.

- Simple Icons 16.28.0 (CC0-1.0): Kia, Mitsubishi, Honda, Citroën, Suzuki, and Tesla.
- CarLogos.org: the transparent full-color PNG emblems used for the remaining brands.

All brand names and logos remain trademarks of their respective owners.

Nissan uses the 2001 mark and Toyota the red 1989 mark to match the supplied visual reference; the remaining sourced PNGs use the standard full-color emblem from the corresponding manufacturer page.

The active Volkswagen, Audi, and BMW card assets (`*-generated*.png`) were regenerated from the user-supplied visual reference with OpenAI's built-in image-generation tool, then cropped, resized, and alpha-normalized for the dark card layout.
